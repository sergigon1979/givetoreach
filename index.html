Give to Reach

I get more first-time email than I can read. A small tool I built sets it aside and answers for me with one request: a $2 gift to GiveDirectly, as a sign that your email was meant for me.

How it works

  1. An email from an address I have never written to is set aside before I see it. No person reads it.
  2. You get a note in the same thread, with a link made for your email.
  3. You give $2 to GiveDirectly on every.org, a US nonprofit donation platform. Every.org sends you the receipt. None of the money comes to me.
  4. Every.org tells Give to Reach that the gift went through, your email comes back to my inbox, and I answer it myself within three business days.

If you are a client, or someone I know

The note reached you by mistake, and I am sorry. Reply to it with one line. I read every answer to the note myself.

If you would rather not

No hard feelings. I will not have seen your email, so read nothing into my silence.

100% of your gift goes to GiveDirectly, through Every.org. Nothing passes through me.

Why $2, and why GiveDirectly

The amount is small on purpose. What I am asking for is a minute of your intent, not your money. GiveDirectly sends money directly to families in extreme poverty, and it is one of the most closely studied charities in the world.

Every.org may offer to add a tip for its own costs. That is optional, and you can set it to zero.

What it keeps about your email

The note has no images and no tracking pixels. Give to Reach reads your email once with an AI model to score how relevant it is to me: a category, a few numbers, and short excerpts with every name, company and link replaced by a placeholder. It keeps those, the code in the link, and a one-way hash of your address. It does not keep your email's text, and it never deletes anything in my mailbox.

The full detail, including the section written for senders, is in the privacy policy.

Questions

Is this spam?
No. It is one reply, in your own thread, to an email you sent me. Nobody who did not write first ever hears from it, and it never writes to the same person twice.
Will you actually reply?
Yes. The tool tracks the promise: when your gift lands, your email is starred in my inbox with a deadline, and I answer within three business days.
Is the money really going to charity?
Yes. The link opens on Every.org, a registered US nonprofit, on GiveDirectly's own page. Every.org takes the gift and issues the receipt. I never see a card and never touch the money.
I gave and heard nothing.
Reply to the note with your receipt. I read every answer to it myself.
