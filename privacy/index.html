Give to Reach is a personal Gmail add-on run by Sergio González. It holds email from senders you do not know, asks them to make a small donation to a charity to have the email restored, and keeps a log of what happened. This page explains what it reads, what it sends where, and what it never does.

What the add-on reads

  • Message headers of new mail in your inbox (sender, recipients, subject, date, message id, authentication results and list headers), to decide whether a sender is already known to you.
  • Your contacts and "other contacts" (people you have emailed), to build the list of known senders. This list stays in your own Google account.
  • The text of held messages only, meaning mail from unknown senders that the add-on has set aside, and only if you switch scoring on. Mail from people you know is never read beyond its headers.

What it sends, and to whom

  • Anthropic (Claude), for scoring, if you switch scoring on: the text of a held pitch, with your own name and address replaced by placeholders. The result is a set of scores and labels. The text is not stored by the add-on after scoring, and Anthropic does not use API traffic to train its models.
  • Every.org, the donation platform: a token unique to the email and the sender's address, so a donation can be matched to the email it restores. Every.org is a registered charity and issues the donor's receipt.
  • The Give to Reach relay at go.givetoreach.com, operated by Sergio González: scores, categories, short excerpts with names, companies and links removed, which price and wording variant was shown, a one-way hash of the sender's address, and event timestamps (reply sent, link opened, donation, restore, your reply). Never the message itself.

What it never does

  • No human other than you reads your mail. No message body is stored by the add-on, the relay or Sergio.
  • Nothing is deleted. Held mail sits under a visible label in your own mailbox.
  • No advertising, no sale of personal data, and no use of your mail to train models.
  • No access to your mail from any server: the add-on runs on Google's servers as you, and the relay holds no Google permission.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Give to Reach's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Published results

Aggregated statistics about cold email received by participants may be published. They never identify a participant or a sender: only pooled counts and rates, with small groups suppressed, and a few hand-picked excerpts with identifying details removed. Participants agree to this when they install, and can opt their data out of anything sold while staying in the free public aggregates.

Retention and control

  • The log the add-on keeps lives in your own Google Drive and is yours to delete.
  • The relay keeps scores and events; IP addresses of link clicks are hashed and purged after 30 days.
  • A kill switch in the add-on stops all activity at once. Uninstalling the add-on revokes its access to your account.
  • Senders can stop all automated replies with one click on the link in the reply.
